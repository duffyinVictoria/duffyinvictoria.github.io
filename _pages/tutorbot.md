Tutor Bot is a Generative AI tool designed to help students revise relevant subject material from Secondary School Mathematics prior to starting tertiary study. 
The implementation below is specific to preparation for MAST10005 (Calculus 1) at the University of Melbourne. 
It reviews only material that is relevant to the subject and does so using language and notation that the students will be expected to use in the subject.
However the overarching prompt can be easily customised to be useful in a variety of mathematical contexts.

[ChatGPT Tutor Bot](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-68773646feb88191ac6a8b813da1977d-title-mast10005-diagnostic-tutor)

A version can also be found in Cogniti under the name _Mathematical Methods Revision Bot for First-Year Mathematics_

The prompt that defines the parameter of the conversation is structured as follows.

1. Define the role of the tutor bot
2. Outline areas to review
3. Define standards and norms for communication
4. Details of areas to review, including sample questions.

The prompt that runs the ChatGPT Tutor Bot can be downloaded here: [Tutor Bot Prompt](/botprompt.txt)
Pasting this prompt into any Generative AI system should yield an experience similar to running the ChatGPT Tutor Bot.
